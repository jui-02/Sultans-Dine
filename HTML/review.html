The Kacchi Biryani at Sultan Dine is simply outstanding! The meat was so tender and flavorful, and the aroma was heavenly. The service was excellent too. This is now my favorite place for authentic Mughlai cuisine in Dhaka.

Fatima Ahmed
Posted on May 28, 2023

Great food overall, especially their mutton rezala and butter naan. The ambiance is nice with traditional decor. The only downside was the waiting time - we had to wait about 30 minutes to get a table on a weekend evening. Would recommend making a reservation.

Karim Uddin
Posted on April 10, 2023

Visited Sultan Dine for a family dinner. The beef tehari and morog polao were excellent. Portions are generous and prices are reasonable for the quality. The staff was attentive and the place was clean. Will definitely come back to try their other dishes.
